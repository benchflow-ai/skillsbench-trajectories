Important functions for testing ipython:
- IPython.core.inputtransformer2.TransformerManager.transform_cell(): Responsible for transforming IPython-specific syntax (like magics) into valid Python code. High risk for parsing issues.
- IPython.core.interactiveshell.InteractiveShell.run_cell(): Executes a cell of code. While it might be too broad for simple fuzzing, it's a critical component.
