The build failed due to a compilation error in the `auto-value` functional integration test.
Specifically, `com.google.auto.value.AutoValueTest` in `google/auto/value/src/it/functional` uses `com.google.common.base.MoreObjects`, which was introduced in Guava 18.0.
However, the integration test module ends up with Guava 17.0 on its classpath, likely because `com.google.truth:truth:0.25` (or 0.24) depends on Guava 17.0 and Maven's dependency mediation picks it over the transitive dependency from `guava-testlib:18.0`.
