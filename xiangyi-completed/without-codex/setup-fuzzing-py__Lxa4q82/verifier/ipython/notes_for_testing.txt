Targets for fuzzing (input parsing and argument splitting):
- IPython.core.splitinput.split_user_input(...) and LineInfo(...) for line parsing.
- IPython.utils._process_common.arg_split(...) for shell-like argument splitting.
- IPython.utils.text.format_screen(...) for line-wrapping logic.

Interesting input dimensions:
- Leading whitespace with escape prefixes (, ; / % ! !! ? ??) and mixed tabs/spaces.
- Magic-like prefixes (%magic, %%cell) and dotted identifiers.
- Quoted strings, backslashes, and unmatched quotes for arg_split.
- Extremely long lines and unicode edge cases (but avoid surrogates).

Potential error types to treat as expected:
- ValueError from arg_split strict parsing.
