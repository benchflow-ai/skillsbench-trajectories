Targets for fuzzing (focus on parsing/formatting and timezone handling):
- arrow.get(...) for strings, timestamps, and date/time inputs (parse paths).
- arrow.parser.Parser().parse(...), parse_iso(...), and _parse_multiformat(...) with varied format strings.
- Arrow.strptime(...) and Arrow.fromtimestamp/utcfromtimestamp with edge timestamps.
- formatter.format(...) for tokenized format strings.
- util helpers: iso_to_gregorian, validate_ordinal, normalize_timestamp.

Interesting input dimensions:
- ISO-8601 variants with/without timezone offsets, fractional seconds, week dates.
- Invalid or ambiguous dates (Feb 29 on non-leap years, 24:00:00, ordinal out of range).
- Timezone expressions (UTC, offsets, tz names), and malformed tz strings.
- Extremely large/small timestamps; negative epochs.

Potential error types to treat as expected:
- ValueError, TypeError, OverflowError, arrow.parser.ParserError.
