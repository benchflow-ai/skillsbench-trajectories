Focus areas for fuzzing
- black.format_str() and format_file_contents(): arbitrary Python snippets, invalid syntax, long lines, comments/strings.
- black.decode_bytes(): odd encodings, invalid byte sequences, mixed newline styles.
- black.assert_equivalent(): AST equivalence checks on formatted output.
- black.files.parse_pyproject_toml(): malformed TOML, missing keys, overrides.
- black.ranges.parse_line_ranges(): malformed ranges, reversed ranges, huge line numbers.
