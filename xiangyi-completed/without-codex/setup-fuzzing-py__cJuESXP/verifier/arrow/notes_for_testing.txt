Focus areas for fuzzing
- arrow.api.get(): parse strings, timestamps, tz offsets, locale strings, and mixed positional/keyword args.
- arrow.parser.Parser.parse()/parse_iso(): ISO-8601 variants, ambiguous dates, invalid tokens, timezone names.
- arrow.formatter.DateTimeFormatter.format() and Arrow.format(): formatting tokens, locale, invalid/unknown tokens.
- Arrow.shift()/replace()/span()/span_range()/floor()/ceil(): DST boundaries, leap days, negative/large offsets.
- Arrow.humanize()/dehumanize(): relative phrases, pluralization, and locale handling.
