Key areas to fuzz (IPython):
- IPython.core.inputtransformer2.TransformerManager.transform_cell / check_complete: cell strings with magics (%, %%), prompts, backslashes, multiline code.
- IPython.core.splitinput.split_user_input: random lines with escapes (!, !!, ?, %%), leading whitespace, unicode.
- IPython.core.magic_arguments.parse_argstring: random arg strings for magic functions.
- IPython.core.completer.Completer.split_line: partial lines and cursor positions.
