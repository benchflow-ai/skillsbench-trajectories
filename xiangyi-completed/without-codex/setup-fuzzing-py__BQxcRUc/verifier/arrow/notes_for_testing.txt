Key areas to fuzz (Arrow):
- arrow.get(...) in arrow/api.py and arrow/factory.py: accepts datetime/date, timestamps, strings with formats, and tzinfo. Fuzz strings (ISO, RFC, custom tokens), numeric timestamps (int/float), and invalid tz names/offsets.
- arrow.parser.Parser.parse / parse_iso in arrow/parser.py: date-time string parsing, timezone offsets, week dates, ordinal dates, leap day handling.
- Arrow.shift / replace / span / range / floor / ceil in arrow/arrow.py: fuzz units (years, months, weeks, days, hours, minutes, seconds, microseconds), large and negative deltas, bounds="()[]".
- Arrow.format and formatter.format in arrow/formatter.py: fuzz format strings (tokens, literals, escapes) and check for exceptions.
- Utility validation in arrow/util.py: validate_ordinal, validate_bounds, normalize_timestamp, iso_to_gregorian.
