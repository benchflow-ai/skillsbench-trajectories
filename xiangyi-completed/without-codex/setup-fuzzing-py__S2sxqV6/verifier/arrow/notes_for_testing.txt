Focus areas for fuzzing
- arrow.api.get / arrow.factory.get: parsing of strings, timestamps, datetime/date, and component tuples.
- Arrow.shift: relative deltas (years/months/days/hours/minutes/seconds) and DST boundary behavior.
- Arrow.format: token parsing and edge cases for format strings.
- Arrow.humanize / Arrow.dehumanize: natural language time deltas and locales.
- arrow.util.normalize_timestamp / iso_to_gregorian / validate_bounds: numeric and bounds handling.
