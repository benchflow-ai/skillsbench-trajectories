**Summary of Approach:**
1.  **Custom Metric Optimization:** The weighted Euclidean distance `d(a, b) = sqrt((w * Δx)² + ((2 - w) * Δy)²)` was implemented by scaling the input coordinates: `x' = w * x`, `y' = (2 - w) * y` and using standard Euclidean DBSCAN. This allowed for efficient vectorization and usage of `scikit-learn`'s optimized routines.
2.  **Grid Search:** A full grid search was performed over `min_samples` (3-9), `epsilon` (4-24, step 2), and `shape_weight` (0.9-1.9, step 0.1).
3.  **Parallelization:** The evaluation was parallelized using `joblib` across available cores to speed up processing of the 847 hyperparameter combinations.
4.  **Evaluation & Matching:** For each image and parameter set, clusters were generated and centroids were greedily matched to expert annotations (closest pairs first, max distance 100). F1 scores were averaged over all expert images, and deltas (match distances) over all images with matches.
5.  **Pareto Frontier:** The results were filtered for F1 > 0.5, and non-dominated solutions (maximizing F1, minimizing delta) were identified and saved.
