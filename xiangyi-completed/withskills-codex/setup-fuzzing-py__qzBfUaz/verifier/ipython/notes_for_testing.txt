Key functions / surfaces to fuzz:
- TransformerManager.transform_cell(): IPython input transformations (%magic, !system, ?help, etc.).
- TransformerManager.check_complete(): completeness/indentation detection for partial cells.
- CompletionSplitter.split_line(): line splitting and delimiter handling.
- magic_arguments.parse_argstring(): argparse-based magic parsing with decorators.
- magic.Magics.parse_options(): getopt-based parsing of magic options.
