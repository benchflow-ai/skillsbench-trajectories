Key functions & inputs to fuzz:
- IPython.core.inputtransformer2.TransformerManager.transform_cell(cell): transforms IPython special syntax.
- TransformerManager.check_complete(cell): determines completeness of code blocks.
- Supporting transforms: cell_magic, ipython_prompt, classic_prompt, escaped commands.
Focus on mixed prompts, magics (%%/%), backslashes, unmatched brackets, and non-ASCII bytes.
