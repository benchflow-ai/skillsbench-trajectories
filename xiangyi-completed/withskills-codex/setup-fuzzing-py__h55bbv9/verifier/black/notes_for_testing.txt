- black.Mode options: line_length, target_versions, string_normalization, is_pyi, is_ipynb, preview/unstable.
- Error paths: InvalidInput, NothingChanged, AST parsing for malformed code.
Focus on odd/partial Python syntax, encoding markers, long lines, and mixed indentation.
