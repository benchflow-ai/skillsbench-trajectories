Key functions & inputs to fuzz:
- ujson.loads / ujson.decode: parse JSON strings with edge cases.
- ujson.dumps / ujson.encode: serialize Python objects, floats, NaN/Inf handling.
- Options: ensure_ascii, escape_forward_slashes, reject_bytes, precise_float, allow_nan.
Focus on malformed JSON, deep nesting, large numbers, and mixed unicode/bytes.
