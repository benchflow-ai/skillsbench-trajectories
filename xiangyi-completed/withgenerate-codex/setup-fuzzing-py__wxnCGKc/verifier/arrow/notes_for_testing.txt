Key targets for fuzzing (arrow):
- arrow.get(...) parsing entrypoint (strings, timestamps, datetime/date objects, tz info).
- arrow.parser.Parser.parse(...) and parse_iso(...), including format tokens and ISO8601 variants.
- Arrow.format(...) and formatter.format(...) for formatting edge cases.
- Arrow.shift(...), floor(...), ceil(...), span(...) and span_range(...) for boundary arithmetic.
- util.normalize_timestamp(...), util.iso_to_gregorian(...), util.validate_bounds(...).

Input ideas:
- Unicode date strings, mixed locale tokens, weird separators, timezone offsets.
- Extreme timestamps (very large/small), leap days, DST transitions.
- Format strings with repeated or invalid tokens.
