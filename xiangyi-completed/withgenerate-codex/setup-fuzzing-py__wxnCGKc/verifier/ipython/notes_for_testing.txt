Key targets for fuzzing (IPython):
- IPython.core.inputtransformer2.TransformerManager.transform_cell(...) for special syntax transforms.
- TransformerManager.check_complete(...) for block completion logic.
- PromptStripper and token transforms (MagicAssign, SystemAssign, EscapedCommand, HelpEnd).

Input ideas:
- Cells with %magic, !shell, ?help, and mixed prompts (>>> ...).
- Multiline inputs with backslashes, brackets, and incomplete blocks.
- Unicode identifiers, weird whitespace, and long single lines.
