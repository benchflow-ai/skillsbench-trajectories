Key entry points / low-dependency utilities
- minisgl.utils.misc.divide_even/divide_up/divide_down: integer division helpers.
- minisgl.utils.registry.Registry.register/__getitem__/supported_names: registry behavior and error paths.
- minisgl.utils.misc.call_if_main: decorator behavior based on name/discard flags.

Fuzzing focus ideas
- Random ints for divide_* (avoid division by zero in the driver).
- Random strings for Registry names; exercise duplicate registration and missing key paths.
- Random names for call_if_main; ensure returned callable behaves.

Things to avoid
- Avoid importing modules that require torch/transformers; stick to utils.* modules.
