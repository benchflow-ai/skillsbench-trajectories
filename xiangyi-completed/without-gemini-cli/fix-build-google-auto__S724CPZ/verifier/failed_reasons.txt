The build failed because the integration test 'functional' in 'auto-value' failed to compile.

This class was introduced in Guava 18.0.
The integration test 'functional' has 'truth' 0.25 and 'guava-testlib' 18.0 as dependencies.
'truth' 0.25 depends on Guava 17.0.
In Maven's dependency resolution, since 'truth' is declared before 'guava-testlib' in the 'functional/pom.xml', its transitive dependency on Guava 17.0 wins over 'guava-testlib''s transitive dependency on Guava 18.0.
Guava 17.0 does not contain 'MoreObjects', leading to the compilation failure.
