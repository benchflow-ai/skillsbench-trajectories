6. KEY FORMULAS AND CALCULATIONS
================================================================================

Cobb-Douglas Production Function:
  Y = A * K^alpha * L^(1-alpha)
  Taking logs: LnY = LnA + alpha*LnK + (1-alpha)*LnL
  TFP residual: LnZ = LnY - alpha*LnK - (1-alpha)*LnL
  In model: LnZ = LnY - alpha*LnK (assuming L is constant)

HP Filter Objective:
  Minimize: Sum((LnZ - LnZ_HP)^2) + Lambda*Sum((D2_LnZ_HP)^2)
  Where D2_LnZ_HP = second-order difference of trend
  Lambda = 1600 (standard for annual data)

Capital Stock Accumulation:
  K(t) = K(t-1)*(1-delta) + I(t)
  K_with includes investment shock: K_with(t) += 812.5 for 2026-2033

Potential GDP Calculation:
  Ystar = exp(LnZ_HP + alpha*LnK + (1-alpha)*LnL)
