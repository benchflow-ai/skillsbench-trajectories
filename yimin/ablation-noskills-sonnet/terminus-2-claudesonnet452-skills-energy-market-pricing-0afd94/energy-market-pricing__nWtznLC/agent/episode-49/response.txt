I think I finally see the issue! When I check the power balance, I'm using:

```python
gen - demand == flow_out - flow_in
```

But this creates n constraints for n buses. In DC power flow, with one angle fixed at the reference bus, we actually have n-1 degrees of freedom. Having n power balance constraints with only n-1 independent equations makes the system overdetermined and potentially inconsistent!

The standard approach is to either:
1. Exclude the reference bus from power balance (let it be the slack)
2. Or include all buses but recognize one constraint is redundant
