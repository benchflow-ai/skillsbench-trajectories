Targets for fuzzing (arrow):
- arrow.get(): parse strings, timestamps, date/datetime inputs, tzinfo handling.
- arrow.Arrow.shift()/replace()/floor()/ceil(): date arithmetic & boundary values.
- arrow.parser.DateTimeParser.parse(): ISO and custom format parsing.
- arrow.formatter.DateTimeFormatter.format(): formatting tokens and locale-sensitive paths.
- arrow.Arrow.humanize()/humanize(): relative time formatting.
- arrow.range()/Arrow.range(): range generation edge cases (intervals, bounds).
