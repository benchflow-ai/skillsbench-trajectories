Key functions & surfaces to fuzz:
- minisgl.message.utils._serialize_any / serialize_type: serialization of nested structures and tensors.
- minisgl.message.utils._deserialize_any / deserialize_type: reconstruction from dicts, handling __type__ markers.
- minisgl.message.{backend,frontend,tokenizer}.encoder/decoder: JSON-compatible message round-trips.
- minisgl.tokenizer.tokenize / detokenize: tokenization of messages (depends on transformers/torch).
Fuzz strategy: focus on message serialization/deserialization with nested dict/list/bytes and fake __type__ data; avoid GPU-only paths.
