Key targets for fuzzing (Arrow):
- arrow.api.get(): parses inputs (strings, timestamps, date/datetime) and tz expressions.
- Arrow.fromtimestamp()/fromdatetime()/fromdate(): conversion edge cases, bounds.
- Arrow.shift(), replace(): mixed units, negative/large deltas.
- Arrow.floor()/ceil()/span()/span_range()/range(): frame rounding and span calculations.
- Arrow.format(): format tokens and locale handling.
- Arrow.humanize(): relative time comparisons across timezones.
- arrow.util.normalize_timestamp(), iso_to_gregorian(), validate_bounds(): boundary math.
Notes:
- Expect ValueError/ParserError on invalid inputs; these are normal.
- Include edge timestamps, leap days, DST transitions, and unusual tz names.
