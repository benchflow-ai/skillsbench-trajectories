Focus areas for fuzzing (date/time parsing + transformations):
- arrow.get(*args, **kwargs) and arrow.factory.ArrowFactory.get: accepts timestamps, datetime/date, ISO strings, and format strings; try mixed inputs, timezone strings, and invalid formats.
- arrow.parser.Parser.parse / parse_iso: parse ISO-8601 variants, week dates, ordinal dates, timezones, fractional seconds, and edge dates (year 1, far future, leap days).
- Arrow.fromtimestamp / fromordinal / fromdatetime: boundary values, negative timestamps, large ordinals, naive vs aware datetimes.
- Arrow.replace / shift / format: shifting across DST boundaries, huge deltas, invalid field names, and invalid locale/format tokens.
- arrow.util.normalize_timestamp / validate_bounds: extreme float values, NaN/inf, and invalid bounds.
