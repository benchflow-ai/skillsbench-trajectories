Key areas to fuzz/test:
- arrow.get(...): parse date/time from strings, timestamps, and naive/aware datetimes; exercises parser + tz parsing.
- Arrow.fromtimestamp()/fromdatetime(): timestamp bounds, timezone handling, fold/ambiguous times.
- Arrow.shift()/replace(): date arithmetic (month/day boundaries, leap years, DST transitions).
- Arrow.format()/strftime()/humanize(): formatting tokens, locale handling, and edge-case values.
- Arrow.span()/span_range(): frame parsing and iteration across boundaries.
