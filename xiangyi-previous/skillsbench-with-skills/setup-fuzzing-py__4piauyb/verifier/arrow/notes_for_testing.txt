Key functions / areas to fuzz in arrow:
- arrow.api.get(): parsing of many input types (str, datetime, date, int/float timestamp, tuple) and timezone/locale options.
- arrow.parser.Parser.parse() and parse_iso(): string parsing for various formats and tokens.
- arrow.formatter.Formatter.format(): formatting datetime with tokens.
- Arrow class methods (arrow/arrow.py): .shift(), .replace(), .format(), .humanize() for edge cases around timezone, offsets, and invalid tokens.
Focus on malformed date strings, unusual locales/timezones, and extreme timestamps.
